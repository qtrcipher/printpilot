        Welcome to PrintPilot

        PrintPilot lets you operate your network printer's menu from this computer — made for printers whose control panel is broken or out of reach. It finds the printer on your network and gives you full keyboard and gamepad control of its menu.

        Everything stays on your local network: LAN-only, no telemetry, no account.
